# Assigned roles and substantive-work handoff

Before substantive work, identify the role assigned by the host configuration
or the current native invocation, its scope, and its model/effort provenance.
Apply this rule to coordinators, advisors, workers and reviewers, including
direct skill invocations. Eligibility is an operational assignment, not a
self-assessment of mathematical ability or a guarantee of correctness.

Substantive work includes interpretation, synthesis, mathematical reasoning,
chat explanations, drafting replacement prose, and editing or deciding changes
to any artifact: research, manuscripts, code, configuration, skills and other
prose. Checking correctness and interpreting validation results also belong to
the substantive worker.

## Establish the assigned role

Use the existing host role configuration or an explicit bounded assignment
made through the host's native invocation. Record the configured role or
assignment source and the actual model selection and exposed reasoning setting.
Matching current-session host metadata or the parent's native selection
conveyed in the packet supplies invocation provenance. A host preset with
explicit model/effort fields also supplies that assignment. A model name or
persona written only in a prompt does not select a model.

For the primary/general-purpose agent, the current user task is an explicit
content/production assignment unless host policy restricts that agent to
evidence/coordination. A separate named production preset is unnecessary.
Record current-session model/effort information when available; if the host
provides none, record it as unknown and continue within this directly assigned
scope. Missing self-metadata alone does not require delegation. A known Luna
selection still carries this fork's evidence/coordination restriction. Proof
and verification duties follow the configured mathematical roles below.

A worker inherits this established assignment; it need not rediscover its
identity or requalify its mathematical ability when local self-metadata is
absent. Record an unsupported reasoning control as unsupported. Keep missing
or conflicting model provenance unknown and resolve it from the host or parent;
do not infer it from a provider catalogue, global default or self-description.

In this user's fork, Luna is assigned literal evidence collection and
coordination only, at every reasoning effort. Increasing effort does not expand
that assignment. Other models follow the configured roles and explicit task
assignments; do not infer permissions from an informal model-family ranking.
An advisor checks the plan, not a model's general mathematical competence.

## Route by the work required

- Literal source collection and coordination use the evidence/coordination role.
- Explaining a supplied argument, synthesizing sources, ordinary drafting and
  artifact editing use a content/production assignment. A coordinator assigned
  that work may carry it out within its scope.
- Constructing a new proof, filling a missing argument, finding a counterexample
  or testing hypotheses use the configured proof role. Use a decomposer when
  there are useful bounded subproblems.
- Before recording a newly established decisive mathematical conclusion as a
  research result or using it as a decisive proof dependency, obtain a separate
  fresh verifier's check of the exact statement and proof, within the owning
  skill's review rules. Routine explanations of supplied arguments, tentative
  reasoning and accurately marked unresolved work follow their assigned scope;
  they do not require this result-adoption check merely to appear in chat.
  A proof role and a verifier may use the same configured model; their
  assignments and contexts must be separate.

A mathematical explanation that needs a missing proof step moves that step to
the proof role. Match the task to these responsibilities and dispatch the
configured role; workers need not first judge whether they or another model
are generally good at mathematics. Model/effort choices are maintained in host
setup and reconsidered when models or task requirements change. Proofs, source
checks and independent verification establish the result's supported scope.

## Hand off and report concrete obstacles

When the work requires a role outside your assignment, use the configured role
through the [subagent invocation contract](subagent-invocation.md). Pass its
assignment and invocation provenance in the bounded packet. If model selection
is unavailable, matching host-session metadata must establish the actual
selection. Resolve conflicting provenance with the parent before proceeding.

The assigned substantive worker owns analysis, replacement text, actual artifact
edits and relevant checks. An evidence-only coordinator may collect literal
evidence and relay that worker's progress and result; it does not produce its
own substantive version or apply the worker's proposed patch.

Escalate or split a bounded subproblem when a hypothesis is missing, a proof
step cannot be discharged, a claimed conclusion conflicts with an example,
permitted sources do not support a needed claim, or the work exceeds the
assigned scope or remaining budget. State the exact obstacle and what has
already been established. Reuse the completed consultation and assignment;
receiving delegated work or feeling uncertain alone does not trigger another
qualification handoff.

If the required role or native dispatch is unavailable, preserve artifacts and
report the established scope, remaining obligations, and missing role or host
support. Do not certify the unresolved claim or manufacture another worker's
eligibility. The owning skill's authorized fallback still applies.

## Behavioral limits

This is a behavioral instruction, not a sandbox or a runtime write barrier.
Skill links and project instructions make it discoverable; they do not prove
compliance. A hook whose host support and behavior have not been validated
does not enforce it. Report observed handoffs and checks separately from any
claim of technical enforcement.
