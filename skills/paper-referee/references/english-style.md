# English-paper conventions

Use `E` IDs for register and typesetting findings. These are this policy's
paper conventions; an explicit author or venue choice takes precedence.
Keep them distinct from mathematical defects (`P4`) and demonstrated reading
failures. They do not govern slides, reading notes, or research plans.

- **E1 Abstract language** — State the contribution and scope in ordinary
  language, without mathematical notation except familiar adjectival forms
  such as "1-Lipschitz". Do not spell a complicated formula out in words.
  Introduce paper-specific terms briefly or omit them. Exact formulas belong
  in the Introduction and body, where they often improve precision.
- **E2 Statements and setup** — A theorem states assumptions and conclusions;
  put constructions and provenance explanations in surrounding prose. Keep
  separate concepts distinguishable in definitions. This does not forbid
  stating a main theorem before developing its proof machinery.
- **E3 Terms and headings** — Italicize a term at its definition. Expand an
  abbreviation at first use and avoid it in headings. The bracket of a
  theorem-class environment carries provenance or an established name, not a
  newly invented descriptive title; a definition may name its defined term.
- **E4 Citations and known facts** — Put the source of a borrowed definition
  or directly quoted result in its heading with an exact locator. Prefer a
  verified citation to reproving routine background; prove any difference
  from that source explicitly. A second proof merits space when its method
  is itself part of the contribution. Keep historical commentary outside proofs.
- **E5 Mathematical typography** — Number displays when later referenced;
  avoid a second number for a statement already identified by its theorem.
  Prefer a formula to opaque prose calculations, while retaining the sentence
  explaining its role. Definitions and labels must remain available after cuts.
- **E6 Register** — Use direct mathematical assertions and ordinary inferential
  language. Flag self-praise, content-free progress narration and ornamental
  names. Prefer sentence breaks to clause-joining semicolons and parenthetical
  em dashes. A phrase search supplies candidates, not automatic violations.
  The default explicit proof ending is "This completes the proof."
- **E7 Reader-facing sources** — Cite scholarly works and public archival
  versions. Keep internal paths, Git hashes and checking history in research
  or review records; use an appropriate public citation for reproducible code.

The paper's mathematical story determines its structure. Do not prescribe a
fixed Introduction sequence, a compulsory example count, or boilerplate
transitions. Existing clear prose is a valid control when assessing a repair.
