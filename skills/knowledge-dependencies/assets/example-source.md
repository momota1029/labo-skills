# Synthetic bounded-sequence argument

This deliberately elementary source tests dependency bookkeeping only.

## Ambient objects

Let x = (x_n) be a sequence of real numbers indexed by natural numbers.
The later conclusion will give a slightly weaker bound on these numbers.
That sentence is a narrative preview of the conclusion.

## Uniform hypothesis

Assume a real M >= 0 satisfies |x_n| <= M for every natural n.

## Estimate

For each natural n, the ambient sequence and uniform hypothesis give |x_n| <= M.

## Conclusion and proof

For every natural n, |x_n| <= M + 1: apply the estimate and M <= M + 1.
The ambient objects and uniform hypothesis are inherited through the estimate.
