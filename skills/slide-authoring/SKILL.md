# Talks and slides

Read [slide standards](references/slide-standards.md). Establish audience,
duration, and one takeaway before choosing density. Produce a compelling
mathematical path through the story, supported by examples and precise
claims where needed.

Use the relevant research plan and Lean evidence, but **never import the
paper-referee rule set wholesale**. A slide can omit technical proofs when it
labels its level of detail honestly and points to a source or lecture note.
Long explanatory material can become `reading/` notes.
